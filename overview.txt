--------------------------------------
  WELCOME TO THE HEMLOCK DICE ROLLER
--------------------------------------
Here's a basic overview of the rules:
The roller uses six-sided dice
You can roll a maximum of 36 dice
The available modifiers are:
Power, Balance, Chance, Leverage,
Reroll, Fudge, Exploding
for Leverage, Reroll, Fudge, and
Exploding, you must input a value.
You will get an error if you don't.
Normally, a 5 or a 6 is 1 "success".
Power: a 6 is two "successes"
Balance: a 4 is a "success"
Chance: only a 6 is a "success"
Leverage: if you have any "successes"
    gain more equal to the value
Reroll: roll all dice equal to or
    less than the value again
Fudge: increase the number on a die
    a number times equal to the value
Exploding: roll additional dice equal
    to the number of dice equal to or
    above the value. Recursive.

That should do it! Ask me (Mathew)
if you have any more questions!
